The commissioned normal `particle-suppression` mode prevents the lot-08 particle excursion, the recovery `closed-loop-fast-4-5` mode prevents the lot-11 latent-electrical excursion, and lot `dram-lot-03` still acquires one critical-dimension defect during normal operation. Eight old Design Runs are invalid under the current execution contract, and the old proposal correctly cannot apply to the already-modified Blueprint.
